- The Vânzări hub shows the price calculator with only Listă de prețuri and Oglindă parteneri; argus opens just the read actions in `SALES_PRICING_ACTIONS` (`_shared/crmRules.ts`) to approved sales/admin users, everything else stays admin-only. Why: sales prices offers without editing rules or saving Argus agents.
- Default cursă of a whole client (no store chosen) follows the country of its stores (`clientDefaultRoute` in `_shared/priceList.ts`): Romania → internal curse average, Bulgaria → BULGARIA, Moldova → the stores' Moldova cursă, other countries → client average; a chosen store keeps its own cursă. Why: the user's default per market.
- Price calculator client scopes (`_shared/priceScope.ts`) read the partner's fiscal code/central name once and inline them as escaped literals; never put correlated partner subqueries in sales WHERE clauses. Why: per-row subqueries pushed large chains past the Senior reader's 25 s limit.
- Semi-finished goods resold as they are (`tradedGood()` in `_shared/priceSourceRules.ts`, list `tradedGoods` in the price rules) are recipe components of kind `marfa`: NIR price like raw material, supply pallets = pieces ÷ configured pieces/pallet, and `tradedGood` products get only the line overhead, never direct labour unless typed. Why: bought-in goods are not processed but still travel on supply trucks.
- Recipe case audit (`_shared/recipeCaseAudit.ts`, rules in `recipeCaseRules.ts`) checks every active Senior recipe (calculator's version choice) and flags version Cantitate 1 whose own components prove several pieces (containers/labels >1, or kg ÷ grams in name); kilo products and odd label counts are separate statuses; CT_CantitateBAX is shown only as context. Why: the user's rule is "components for N pieces but lei/bax = lei/buc"; invoice case content alone gave false positives.
- Offers to another client (`OfferToClient.tsx`, action `price_history`) reuse the price_list engine for the target partner (its cursă and network fee) plus the target's whole Senior invoice history, matched to the target's own article by family + grams without channel suffixes (`_shared/offerMatch.ts`; MENTA 30 MC → MENTA 30 MC MD, looser matches labelled "asemănător"); any finished product can be added to the offer; Moldova points without trips of their own cursă take the Moldova cursă that has trips (the Linella truck). Why: export offers need real transport and past prices, and each client buys under its own Senior article.
- Price calculator real selling price (`recentSale`, per-store sales, profit real) is the average of the chosen calendar month only (`saleWindow` in `_shared/priceDefaults.ts`; running month = 1st to today), while transport windows keep their own rolling days. Why: the user checks whether keeping a given month's price makes a profit.
- Chat and live price reports use `costMonth` in `priceDefaults.ts` for unspecified months and retain explicit choices; tool formula metadata describes the sheet's actual sales-share calculation. Why: changing the answer surface must not change its cost period or explain a different calculation.
- Generic application reads verify one page beyond an exact safety ceiling and fail on extra rows; `boundedQueryResult` discloses output truncation to chat, agents and CEO assistant. Why: a limited row/group subset must not become a fabricated total.
- Warehouse forecast reads live in `purchasingForecast.ts` and schema reads in `businessSchema.ts`; Argus keeps its compatibility export, while ERP imports shared modules directly. Why: separate edge deployments bundle `_shared`, not sibling function directories.
- Washed-mix labour speed (`_shared/priceLineSpeed.ts`) is measured only on production products with the same pack format (PG/GASTRO/VRAC/KG…), on the line kind where the product is really packed (Vracuri/GASTRO vs machines), with each moment of a line shared between the sessions open then by pieces/hour; too few timed sessions → that line's norm; mixes packed on Vracuri/GASTRO use the „Vracuri / Gastro” rule line. Why: GASTRO sessions left open all day gave a 500 PG mix ~15 buc/h and ~23 lei/buc labour with the 13-person multihead crew.
