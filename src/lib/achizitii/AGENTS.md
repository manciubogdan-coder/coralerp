- Purchasing radar (`/administrativ/radar-achizitii`, rules in `src/lib/achizitii/truckRadar.ts` + `_shared/truckRadarRules.ts`) takes its need from the Achiziții forecast rows (`ClientOrdersForecast onRows`): need = 4-week same-weekday average consumption until the next arrival (stock not subtracted), difference = projected stock at arrival + ordered − need; each warehouse has its own rules (deadline from the truck schedule or from each product's forecast lead time) stored per type in server-only Cloud `achizitii_radar_setari`, observed order versions in `achizitii_comenzi_istoric`, via argus `radar_*`; past days are replayed with `ordersAsOf`/`rowAsOf` (morning stock, only earlier movements/orders, 4-week average before that day). Why: one forecast rule set, no hindsight in replays, and supplier orders in the legacy DB keep no edit history.
- The radar page is mounted at `/administrativ/radar-achizitii` and `/achizitii/radar`; argus opens only its reads (`PURCHASING_RADAR_ACTIONS`) to approved Achiziții/admin users, saving radar rules stays admin-only. Why: the purchasing team sees what the manager sees but cannot change the rules it is checked against.
- Truck composer (`src/lib/achizitii/truckBuilder.ts` `composeWeek`, two-week panel on the MP forecast): one truck per arrival day (`isArrivalDay`, Tue–Sun: loading Mon–Sat, 1 day transit), goods for production day D ride on `arrivalFor(D)` (last arrival before D, so Sunday covers Mon–Tue and goods are never used on their arrival day), need per day = sequential projection shortfall vs buffer, pallets by kg/palet norms; tiny days (<3 pal) merge into the previous truck; a truck over the maximum spills back onto earlier open trucks (not today/tomorrow) — sturdy goods (heads/radicchio ≥400 kg/pal) up to `MAX_AGE_STURDY` days before use, perishables only onto the truck right before and at most `MAX_AGE_PERISHABLE` days old; trucks under the minimum pull sturdy goods ≤2 days earlier; each move can be refused by id. Manual truck edits replace only that truck's still-to-order goods, live in browser storage until the order is generated (`TruckOrderDialog`: one `product_orders` row per product with its last supplier, delivery = arrival day, one Excel sheet per supplier). Why: one daily truck instead of extra Saturday trucks, without bringing herbs/leaves days early.
- The MP/daily forecast column "Comandă sugerată" of day D shows `dailyPlan` (truckBuilder) for arrival on D+1 — the same sequential plan as the truck panel (orders already placed count, earlier suggestions subtracted). Why: the table and the trucks must never recommend different quantities.
- Supplier price audit (tab „Furnizori și prețuri” on the radar page, rules in `src/lib/achizitii/supplierPriceAudit.ts`): agreed EUR prices per product × supplier live in server-only Cloud `achizitii_preturi_furnizori` (append-only, latest row active; read by Achiziții, saved admin-only via argus `supplier_prices*`); argus `supplier_price_audit` reads Senior FF/FFI/FFIC lines live (EUR price as billed, lei lines ÷ BNR rate of the invoice day); each line splits extra cost into supplier overcharge (billed − agreed) and supplier choice (agreed − cheapest listed). Why: show both who changed prices and what buying elsewhere cost, without hiding credit notes.
