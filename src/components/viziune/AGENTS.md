- Post effects live in `Effects.tsx` (switchable, dropped on PerformanceMonitor fallback; no N8AO, it crashes on this three version). Components that spread props onto three objects (drei Sky, postprocessing effects) are created with `createElement`, not JSX. Why: the dev tagger's data-* props crash them.
- Walk mode: movement, collisions and crosshair picking (machines first, then warehouse/office areas from `walkAreas`, hidden behind walls) are pure rules in `src/lib/viziune/walk.ts`; `Walker.tsx` only reads input and moves the camera in place of OrbitControls; scene clicks are ignored while walking (`walkContext`). Why: walking can be tested without a GPU and stray clicks must not open panels.
- Warehouse panels (`WarehouseDetail.tsx`, rules in `src/lib/viziune/warehouseState.ts`): raw-material lots from inventory rows with kg/pallet from the price rules; Picking per store → product with Senior pieces/pallet (selling unit 28), preferring the store's own Senior code from `productie_baxare_magazine`; sizes come from admin-only argus `viziune_pallets`, read only for products/names on screen; products without a size are listed, never counted. Why: pallet counts must come from Senior/the rules, not guesses.
